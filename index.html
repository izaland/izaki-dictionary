About the Izaki Language Resource Site

Welcome to the Izaki Language Resource Site — your central hub for exploring Izaki, the constructed language developed for Izaland, a fictional nation within the collaborative worldbuilding project OpenGeoFiction.

This platform offers a growing collection of tools, including a multilingual dictionary, writing-system references, and grammar resources, designed both for learners and for contributors to Izaland's cultural and linguistic world.

Izaki is developed by Izaland's mapper ITC, shaped as an a priori language with a structural blend of influences:

  • Japanese: writing system conventions, topic–comment structure, sentence order
  • Finnish: noun declensions, morphosyntactic patterns, phonetic tendencies

Despite these inspirations, Izaki stands as a unique linguistic system with its own grammar logic and cultural depth within the world of Izaland.
