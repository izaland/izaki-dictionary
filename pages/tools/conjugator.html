Grammatical notes / 文法筆記（動詞）

In Izaki, the dictionary form of verbs ends in -su (𖬖𐐢); the material before it is the lexical stem used to build other forms.

1) Persons / 人稱
  • Singular: 1st -n（我）, 2nd -s（汝）, 3rd ∅（彼）.
  • Plural: 1st -nne（我等）, 2nd -sse（汝等）, 3rd -han（彼等）.
2) Verb groups / 動詞三類
  • Group 1: ends in CV + su (…-asu, -esu, -isu, -osu, -usu).
  • Group 2: ends in VV + su (long vowel / two vowels before -su).
  • Group 3: consonant + su (often n/s/l/r/k/h + su), with stem alternations.
3) Negative / 否定（無）

The negative marker is na（無; often written 𖭑） placed close to the stem; personal endings attach after it.

Example: luma-n “I sleep” → luma-na-n “I don’t sleep”.

4) Tenses shown here / 本頁所示時制
  • Present / 現在形: stem + person ending.
  • Simple past / 過去形: past base + person ending; past negative commonly uses -nai right after the stem.
  • Perfect / 完了形: -le after the (strong) stem.
  • Future / 未來形: -ma after the (strong) stem; functionally “expectative”.

Note: some Group 1 verbs have a “weak stem” vs “strong stem”; the weak stem is associated with a devoicing/purification pattern (清音化) and is discussed in the grammar tradition as 弱體.
